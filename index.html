AI Shorts Uploader

A personal tool that publishes and monitors YouTube Shorts on its owner's own channels.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules short educational animal-fact videos to channels owned by the developer.
  • Reads view counts, watch-time analytics and comments for those same channels to produce a daily performance report.

Who it is for

This app is used only by its developer to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Google user data

The app requests YouTube access only for the channels its owner signs in with. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed, how it is used and how to revoke access.

Privacy Policy

AI Shorts Uploader · Effective November 11, 2026

Scope

AI Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users. This policy explains how the app handles Google user data.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app may request these permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: channel and video details, such as titles, publish schedules, view and subscriber counts.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: YouTube Analytics reports, such as watch time, average view percentage and traffic sources.
  • youtube.force-ssl: uploading and scheduling videos, and reading comments, on the owner's own channels.

How the data is used

  • To upload and schedule the owner's videos.
  • To generate a daily performance report for the owner's channels.
  • Report contents (statistics and video titles) may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to write a summary of the report. No Google account credentials are ever sent.

The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to anyone except as described above.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers and are never published.
  • Reports and collected statistics are stored in the owner's GitHub repository.
  • Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for these reports and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading or uploading anything.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
